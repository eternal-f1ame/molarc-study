MolARC-9 Human Study

What this is. A set of grid puzzles built from real molecules. Every puzzle shows two or three demonstration pairs (an input grid and its output grid) that follow one hidden rule, then a test input. Your job: work out the rule from the demonstrations and fill in the output grid for the test input.

The grids. A grid is 10 × 10. A molecule with n atoms occupies rows and columns 0 to n-1: atom tokens on the diagonal, bond tokens off the diagonal (symmetric), an end marker (token 1) on row n and column n, zeros elsewhere. Bonds are single (12), double (13) or triple (14). Hydrogens are not drawn. The legend under the editor names every token.

The rules of the game. You get two attempts per puzzle. After the first attempt the page tells you whether it matches the reference answer. Take as long as you need; the time per puzzle is recorded. Two practice puzzles with visible answers come first. Your name and email, your answers and the time per puzzle are stored in a results sheet. Name and email are used only to contact you about the study and are never published; results are reported under anonymous ids.
